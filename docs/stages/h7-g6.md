(`docs/research/plan.md`); items 1 and 3 of `docs/decisions/measurement-protocol.md` §13. Directive #74.

**This declaration is committed before any number is computed** (acceptance criterion 1). The code

## 1. What is declared here, and where the protocol decides

The protocol decides the goalposts (§3, §4, §5), the test (§6), the verdict and the spending (§9) and the missing-day
rules (§11). This document decides only what the protocol leaves to the power study: the days, the planting, the grid,
the windows, the draws, the seed and the reading rule. Where the protocol is silent the module refuses rather than
choosing (see the marked questions, §8).

## 2. The days and the labels

- **R and S** are read through `repo_liquidity.h7_estimator.asof_days`, that is through `repo_model.asof.InformationRule`
  with `scarcity.measurement_declaration()`, on the panel version 1 rebuilt to its manifest. R is
  `scarcity.reserves_ratio` and S is `scarcity.reserve_scarcity_state` of the reads at the decision instant. Nothing is
  rebuilt here. No day after 2026-09-03 is read (the panel ends there).
- **The event** on T is the material-use event: the Fed's repo operations accepted on T of at least $1.0bn
  (`fed_repo_accepted >= 1.0`, `docs/decisions/data-meaning.md`, answer 14), from `repo_model.fed_liquidity.assemble` at
  the pin, on the parent's tracked repo-operation snapshots, cut at the panel's build cutoff.
- **The scored days** are the days from 2019-09-17 (`deployable.EVENTS_FROM`) with an event label, a ratio and a state.
  The parent's snapshots end on 2025-12-31 and `assemble` refuses a later date (`StaleReadError`), so **the labelled
  days end on 2025-12-31, not 2026-09-03.** R and S are still read to 2026-09-03; the 2026 days carry no label and are
  not scored (marked question Q1). A day whose label, ratio or state is missing is dropped and counted.
- **The lockbox** (`repo_model.lockbox`): `asof_days` calls `require_unlocked`. The near-blind tier is opened (the
  parent's `metadata/lockbox.json`, Eleonora's GO #151), and no day of the blind tier exists in the panel.

## 3. The statistic (protocol §5), as the module computes it

For an estimate E (ranked low to high, an event expected to sit low), the stratum s is one value of S. For each
stratum with at least one event and at least one other day, `gain_s = AUROC_s(E) − AUROC_s(R)`, ties counting half. The
pooled statistic is the mean of `gain_s` weighted by the number of events in the stratum. A stratum with no event or no
other day is dropped, and the number dropped is reported. R scores exactly 0.

## 4. The test (protocol §6) and the looks (protocol §9)

- **Bootstrap.** The parent's `metrics.stationary_bootstrap_indices` over the window's days, the event indicator, E, R
  and S resampled together, mean block 10 business days. The strata and their weights are recomputed on each resample.
- **Looks.** 60, 125 and 250 days (`scoring_protocol.CHECKPOINTS`). The lower bound at look k is the quantile of the
  bootstrap at the one-sided nominal level `scoring_protocol.nominal_levels()[k]` (the parent's Lan–DeMets
  O'Brien–Fleming boundaries, read, not rebuilt): 6.3e-5, 5.6e-3 and 4.8e-2.
- **Draws.** 100,000 at looks 60 and 125 and 10,000 at look 250: the model repo's ruling of 13 October 2026
  (`scoring_protocol.INTERIM_DRAWS`, PR #660), because 10,000 draws cannot resolve 6.3e-5. A level the draws cannot
  resolve is refused, never rounded.
- **Seed.** `20261014 + the window's first index`. (The registration fixes the real seed; this is the study's.)
- **Supported at a look** (§9): the lower bound is above 0, the point is at or above the MME (0.05), and at least 10
  events are in the window. Goalposts 1 and 2 are assumed to hold: the planted estimates are not given an interval
  width or a refit history, so those two are not tested here.

## 5. The planted signal

`E_t = R_t + e_t − c · σ · y_t`, where `y_t` is the event indicator, `σ` the standard deviation of R over the scored
days, and `e_t` independent Normal(0, (0.1 σ)²) noise that breaks ties. A positive `c` lowers E on event days.

- **c is calibrated** by bisection on the whole scored span (2019-09-17 to 2025-12-31) so that the §5 statistic of E
  against R equals the target gain, to 1e-4. The calibration noise is drawn once with seed 20261014. Each replayed
  window redraws `e_t` with its own seed, so the realised gain in a window differs from the target, as a real one would.
- **The grid of target gains:** 0 (the null, for size), 0.02, 0.035, 0.05 (the MME), 0.065, 0.08, 0.10, 0.125, 0.15.
- A target the calibration cannot reach (the statistic cannot exceed what AUROC allows) is reported as unreachable.

## 6. The windows

- **Lengths:** 60, 125 and 250 consecutive scored days. A window starts at every 20th scored day and must fit entirely
  inside the scored span.
- **Era** is the era of the window's first day: to 2020, 2021–24, 2025 on. The pooled figure is reported beside the three.
- **Per window:** the number of events; for each grid value, the lower bound, the point, and whether it is supported.
- **Days to the event floor:** from every 20th scored day, the number of scored days until 10 events have been logged;
  the median and the 90th percentile by era, and the count of starts that never reach 10 inside the span.

## 7. The reading rule

- **Power** at a length and a gain is the share of windows supported. Windows overlap, so its 95% interval is the Wilson
  interval at `n_eff = n_windows × 20 / length` (at most `n_windows`), not at the number of windows.
- **Size** is the same share at gain 0.
- **The MME is detectable** at a length if the power at 0.05 is at least 0.80. If it is not at 250 days, the study reports
  the smallest grid gain whose power at 250 days is at least 0.80, or says that none is.
- **Nothing here changes the MME or the event floor.** They become marked questions with the result (§8).

## 9. The second record (directive #106): the corrections of finding #95

The power study of PR #76 is archived and a second record is published beside it; nothing in the first is edited. This
section is committed before any number of the second record is computed. It changes how §4-§7 are carried out, not what
they declare, and each point is a finding of #95 or a consequence of the pin moving to `8fd2cf8` (L27, #105).

1. **The event floor counts the events in the window.** §4 says "at least 10 events are in the window", and protocol §9
   says "10 stress events logged". The first record counted the events of usable strata only (`test.events`). The
   second counts every event in the window (`InformationTest.window_events`) for the supported rule and for "windows at
   the floor", and keeps the usable-stratum count as a second column, labelled. A window whose one stratum has no day
   without an event (the 60-day window from 2019-09-17: 60 events, 0 usable) is a window at the floor.
2. **Two readings, each with its interval and its size.** The pooled reading (every window) and the reading "among windows
   at the floor" (the decision reading, Q6). For each, the power at every grid gain with its 95% Wilson interval at the
   effective number of windows (`goalposts.power`; the count of windows of that reading, times 20, over the length), and
   the size, which is the supported share at gain 0. The tables give the count of windows of each reading beside every
   share. The headline names the reading of every figure.
3. **The seed is the declared one:** `20261014 +` the window's first index (`goalposts.window_seed`), for the bootstrap
   draws and for the planted noise of that window. The first record's `SEED + start*10 + look` is retired.
4. **The calibration meets §5's 1e-4.** The bisection ends on the end of its last bracket nearer the target, and
   `goalposts.calibrate` refuses a target it cannot meet within `goalposts.CALIBRATION_TOLERANCE` (1e-4). The record
   lists each target's realised gain on the whole span.
5. **The labels run to 2026-09-03.** The parent's repo-operation snapshots are extended to that date at the new pin
   (`repo_model.fed_liquidity.declared_coverage_end`). The event is unchanged: the Fed's repo operations accepted on the
   day of at least $1.0bn (Q1, decided by Eleonora on 15 October 2026: data-meaning answer 14's event). No day after
   2026-09-03 is read.

## 10. The second record, Part B (avenue #98): power at the 2025-26 event rate

The first record's events come in two clusters (2019-20 and 2025). Part B asks how the test would do if events kept
arriving at the 2025-26 rate and in the 2025-26 clusters. **Declared here, before any run of Part B.**

- **The event process.** A two-state Markov chain on the daily event indicator (`goalposts.fit_event_process`):
  `start` is the share of event days among the days that follow a quiet day, `stay` the share among the days that follow
  an event day. It is fitted to the observed event indicator of the labelled days from 2025-01-01 to 2026-09-03
  (consecutive labelled days; the parameters and the transition counts are recorded). A simulated series starts from the
  chain's stationary share (`goalposts.simulate_events`).
- **Days to the floor.** 2,000 simulated series of 1,000 days each, series `k` seeded `20261014 + 100000 + k`. The record
  gives, for the days until 10 events have been logged (`goalposts.days_to_events`, from day 0, "a 2025 start"), the
  share of series that do not reach 10 within 1,000 days, and the mean, median and 90th percentile of those that do.
  The mean is of the reaching series and is labelled so.
- **Power at 250 days.** 400 replicates, replicate `k` seeded `20261014 + k` (the planted noise and the bootstrap use this
  integer; the replicate's event series is drawn from `random.Random(f"events:{20261014 + k}")`, a separate stream, so
  that the simulated events are independent of the planted noise). Replicate `k` takes the first 250 labelled
  days from 2025-01-01 (their real R and S), replaces their events by a simulated series of 250 days, plants the signal
  of each grid gain, and runs the §4 test at the third look (10,000 draws, nominal level `nominal_levels()[2]`) with the
  supported rule of §4 and point 1 of §9.
  **Planting (amended before any Part B result; the first draft is superseded).** The first draft reused Part A's shifts.
  A 12-replicate smoke run of the script showed that this overshoots: Part A's shifts are calibrated on days whose events
  R already separates, and events simulated independently of R leave R no AUROC to lose, so a shift worth 0.05 in Part A
  planted a mean gain of about 0.17. Part B therefore calibrates its own shift for each grid gain, with the same
  machinery (`goalposts.calibrate_over`, the bisection and the 1e-4 tolerance of §9 point 4): the shift for which the mean
  §5 gain over 200 reference series equals the target. Reference series `j` is the same 250 real-R days with an event
  series drawn from `random.Random(f"events:{20261014 + 200000 + j}")` and noise seed `20261014 + 200000 + j`; none is
  a replicate. A replicate's own gain then varies around the target, as a window's does in Part A. The smoke run's
  figures are not used.
- **Reading.** Replicates are independent, so the 95% Wilson interval is taken at the 400 replicates. The record gives
  power at each grid gain, the size (the share at gain 0), and the same two readings as point 2 of §9 (all replicates;
  among replicates whose 250 days hold at least 10 events), with the count of replicates of each beside the shares.
  It gives the mean realised statistic at gain 0. It picks no reading and changes neither the MME nor the floor.
- **What it does not show.** The simulated events are independent of R and S, so the null is R with no relation to the
  events, and the planted signal is the same oracle shift as in Part A. A chain fitted to 2025-26 clusters carries the
  clustering of two years; whether the next year clusters likewise is not tested.
- **Q7. The process (marked question for Eleonora; nothing is settled in code).** Whether a two-state chain fitted to
  2025-01-01 to 2026-09-03 is the process to plan the confirmation window against, or whether a different calibration
  window, a longer memory, or events tied to settlement and quarter-end dates should replace it. Part B is a
  planning figure, not an input to the verdict, the MME or the floor (Q2 and Q6 stay hers).
